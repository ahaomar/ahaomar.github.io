402 million people displaced by disasters since 2008 – most within their own borders.

That is not a projection. It is the recorded figure, from 2008 to 2023, drawn from the World Bank and UNHCR.

When floods, storms and droughts force people to leave their homes, the data is often invisible until it is mapped. I built an interactive dashboard to make displacement visible, comparable, and explorable – country by country, year by year.

"Displaced by Disaster – Who Extreme Weather Moves" draws on open World Bank Indicators API data to show:

• New disaster displacement per country, from 2008 through 2023
• Year-selectable choropleth map – see how the scale shifts annually
• Per-country trends highlighting the worst year on record, climate exposure, and links to refugee data (UNHCR)
• Auto-generated insights – no reading between the lines

In 2023 alone, 26.4 million people were newly displaced by disasters worldwide.

The dashboard is a single static HTML/JS page – hosted on GitHub Pages, no backend, no database. Live data, automatically refreshed.

I built this because I believe policy intelligence should be public, accessible, and grounded in official data.
