I built a dashboard that visualises 26.4 million people displaced by disasters in a single year – using only open data and a static HTML page.

The challenge: how do you communicate the scale of climate-induced displacement to decision-makers in a way they can *see* and *explore*?

"Displaced by Disaster – Who Extreme Weather Moves" is a data brief I built on GitHub Pages:

• Choropleth map of people newly displaced by floods, storms and droughts (2008–2023)
• Year selector – the map, totals and trends update instantly
• Click any country to see its displacement timeline, peak year, climate-risk context, and refugee data (UNHCR)
• Auto-generated takeaways – no manual interpretation

All data comes live from the World Bank Indicators API. No backend. No database. Just client-side JavaScript, Leaflet, and Chart.js.

402 million people have been displaced by disasters since 2008. The data is public. Making it *usable* is a design choice.
