Focus areas

Drupal governance platforms

Multi-tenant, federated Drupal where data sovereignty and cross-agency collaboration have to coexist. Custom modules that extend core without closing off the upgrade path — because a UN platform has to survive staff turnover, budget cycles and a decade of production life.

Proven on

  • UN Migration Network Hub Drupal 10 governance platform for the Global Compact for Migration, integrating dozens of UN agencies and serving 195 member states.
  • Bionorica global multi-site Drupal 8 on Pantheon, dozens of country and brand sites from one codebase, with a governance model that let non-technical regional teams publish safely.

Data, GIS and evidence-to-decision pipelines

Ingesting sources that disagree with each other, harmonising them without flattening provenance, and rendering the result so a policymaker reaches a defensible reading quickly. Spatial queries at the scale where naïve approaches stop returning.

Proven on

  • CLIMB & the Human Mobility and Climate Change Database PostGIS-backed data-for-policy platform turning multi-source displacement research into queryable intelligence for G7 delegations.
  • Global Migration Data Portal Interactive dashboards and visualisation modules over dozens of statistical sources.
  • Five independent dashboards Built against the live public APIs of UNHCR, UNFCCC, the World Bank and Our World in Data.

AI in content operations, with guardrails

Adding AI to an established editorial workflow without surrendering editorial control. The pattern I use: the model reads structured data first, generates language second, and a human approves before anything publishes — with an audit trail that survives scrutiny.

Read the field note

Components

  • RAG grounded in verified sources Retrieval over the institution's own vetted corpus, so generated text can be traced to a document rather than to a model's memory.
  • Human-in-the-loop approval gates Nothing reaches a public surface without a named reviewer, which is a governance requirement before it is a technical one.
  • Provenance preserved through the pipeline Every derived field keeps a pointer back to its source record.

Field systems under real constraints

Building for operations that run from a well-equipped Geneva headquarters to a low-connectivity field site in a conflict-affected region — on the same platform, with the same data integrity guarantees.

Proven on

  • Ahlan Simsim mobile API backend Drupal REST backend for a UNICEF-funded programme, deployed across multiple conflict-affected countries on intermittent connections.
  • Secure field data portal Role-based portal letting IRC field staff analyse programme data and reallocate resources in real time.
