LINKEDIN POST — "Ten Years of Flight: Where Refugees Come From"
For: UNHCR, IOM, humanitarian and refugee-policy audiences, hiring managers
============================================================

28,000 Ukrainian refugees abroad in 2021. 5.7 million a single year later.

That is what a crisis looks like in the data — and it is exactly why I built an interactive decade of displacement.

"Ten Years of Flight: Where Refugees Come From" animates UNHCR's official refugee statistics from 2015 to 2024 on a live world map:

• Press Play and watch a decade unfold — Ukraine 2022 erupts onto the map, Afghanistan doubles after 2021, and the long Syrian displacement slowly recedes
• Refugees tracked from both sides: by country of origin (117 countries) and by host country (105 countries)
• Click any country to see its full decade — who left, who it shelters, peak year, largest single-year jump
• Auto-generated takeaways, computed live from the data — no reading between the lines
• Top-8 origins table for every year, 2015 → 2024

Some of what the map shows:

From 16.1 million refugees worldwide in 2015 to over 31 million by 2024 — nearly doubling while the world watched.

The three largest origins in 2024: Syria (5.95M), Afghanistan (5.77M) and Ukraine (5.12M) — three crises, three very different timelines, all visible in a single scrub.

Türkiye hosts 2.94 million refugees. Iran 3.49 million. The burden of protection is not evenly shared — the map makes that impossible to miss.

All data streams live from the UNHCR Population Statistics API on every page load. One static HTML/JS page — GitHub Pages, no backend, no database. Leaflet, Chart.js, and official UN data.

I built this because refugee data should not stay locked in PDFs and press releases. It should be explorable — by policymakers, researchers, and anyone who wants to understand the decade.

Explore it here: https://ahaomar.github.io/dashboards/unhcr/ten-years-of-flight/index.html
