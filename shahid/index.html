No. 01 — Counsel

About

Justice is procedural before it is anything else — get the filing, the framework, and the facts right, and the outcome follows.

Shahid Ashfaq Sulehri is an Advocate of the High Court based in Lahore, with over two decades of experience spanning civil, criminal, corporate, and property litigation. Since 2004 he has run an independent practice, Sulehria's Legalis, representing clients across a broad range of courts and disputes.

Since 2012, he has also served as Manager of Legal Administration & Public Regulatory Affairs at Pharmedic Laboratories (Pvt) Ltd., directing compliance, audits, and legal policy for a licensed pharmaceutical manufacturer — regulatory experience he now extends to a small roster of retainer clients in pharma, healthcare, welfare, and trade.

He was elected Vice President of the Bar Association Zafarwal-Narowal for 2023–2024, and continues to train and mentor junior lawyers in litigation procedure and courtroom advocacy.
