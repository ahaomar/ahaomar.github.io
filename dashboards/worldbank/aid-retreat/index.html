    Data Sources & Methodology

    Official development assistance

    World Bank Indicators API — DT.ODA.ODAT.CD: net ODA received, current US$, World aggregate, 2000–2023.

    Displaced population

    UNHCR Population Statistics API — global aggregate of refugees, asylum-seekers, internally displaced people and others of concern, 2015–2024.

    Method

    Aid per displaced person = ODA received (World) divided by UNHCR people of concern (World). Both series are fetched live on page load; all takeaways are computed from the responses, never hard-coded.
