    Data Sources & Methodology

    Renewable freshwater & water stress

    Internal renewable freshwater resources per capita (cubic metres per person per year) and freshwater withdrawal as a share of total renewable resources. FAO AQUASTAT via the World Bank Indicators API: ER.H2O.INTR.PC, ER.H2O.FWTL.ZS.

    Urban population growth

    Annual percentage growth rate of urban populations, UN World Urbanization Prospects via the World Bank Indicators API: SP.URB.GROW.

    API & Licensing

    Retrieved live from the World Bank Indicators API v2 under CC BY 4.0; boundaries from the open world.geo.json dataset. Water stress above 25% is internationally recognised as high; above 70%, critical.
