Data Sources & Methodology

Climate exposure

World Bank Indicators API — EN.CLC.MDAT.ZS: droughts, floods and extreme temperatures, % of population affected, average 1990–2009 (single benchmark).

Disaster displacement

World Bank Indicators API — VC.IDP.NWDS: internally displaced persons, new displacement associated with disasters, 2015–2023.

Method

162 countries have both series. Quadrants split at exposure = 3% and displacement = 0.5% of population. Bubble size ∝ population. All takeaways computed live from the data.
