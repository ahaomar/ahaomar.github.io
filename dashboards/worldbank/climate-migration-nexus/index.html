    Data Sources & Methodology

    Disaster displacement

    World Bank Indicators API — VC.IDP.NWDS: people newly displaced within their own country by disasters such as floods, storms and droughts, 2008–2024.

    Climate context

    World Bank Indicators API — EN.CLC.MDAT.ZS: share of population affected by droughts, floods and extreme temperatures (annual average 1990–2009), shown in the country detail panel.
