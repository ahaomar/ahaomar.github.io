Beyond the Breaking Point: the gains now on the line

In July 2026 UN Women warned that more than a million women and girls have already lost access to critical support as donors retreat, with 120 million women and girls needing assistance. Behind that warning sit four long-run series — work, maternal health, girls’ schooling and political power — and the aid flows that finance them. Drag from 1990 to 2025 and watch what three decades of progress actually rest on. Live from the World Bank.

      Data Sources & Methodology

      Indicators

      World Bank Indicators API — SL.TLF.CACT.FE.ZS (female labour force participation) and SL.TLF.CACT.MA.ZS (male), SH.STA.MMRT (maternal mortality ratio), SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.FE.ZS (lower-secondary completion, female), SG.GEN.PARL.ZS (women in parliament), 1990–2025. Aggregates are excluded — only the 217 World Bank member economies are mapped.

      Money backdrop

      DT.ODA.ODAT.CD, net official development assistance received by the world, 1990–2023 (latest available in the World Bank series).

      Method

      Reporting years differ by indicator, so the map carries the latest value reported on or before the selected year and labels the year it came from. Rankings, callouts, the world line and every takeaway are computed from the API response on page load — no figure is hard-coded.
