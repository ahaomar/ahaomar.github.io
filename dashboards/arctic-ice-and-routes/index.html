    Route thresholds

    Northern Sea Route is classed from the September minimum: extended season ≤ 4.0M km2, open ≤ 5.0, marginal ≤ 6.0, otherwise closed. Northwest Passage: open ≤ 4.0, marginal ≤ 4.8, otherwise closed. These are published navigability thresholds, not observed traffic.

    Ice-free window (modelled)

    Days below 7.0M km2 are estimated by fitting a cosine through that year's March maximum and September minimum. It is a seasonal proxy derived from two real observations per year — not a count of observed shipping days.
