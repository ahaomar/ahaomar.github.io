Why methane matters now the short game of climate policy
The 20-year view. Over 20 years, one tonne of methane warms the planet 82× more than one tonne of CO2. Over 100 years, still 29×. Methane lives in the atmosphere for roughly a decade — CO2 for centuries.
The political consequence. Because CH4 decays fast, cutting it pays back within a decade — it's the only lever that visibly slows warming before 2050. That's why the Global Methane Pledge (COP26, 2021) targets a 30% cut by 2030.
