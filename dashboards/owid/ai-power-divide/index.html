The AI Power Divide: who powers the boom, who waits

As COP31 in Antalya puts AI’s electricity appetite on the agenda for the first time, the split underneath is older than the boom: data centres already take 4.9% of all electricity in the United States, while roughly 657 million people — 8% of humanity — still have no power at all.       Data Sources & Methodology

      Data-centre electricity share

      Our World in Data, based on the IEA Energy and AI analysis — 10 regions and economies, estimates for 2020, 2023, 2024 and 2025 (2021–2022 are not estimated).

      Electricity access & population

      World Bank Indicators API — EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS: access to electricity, % of population, 2000–2024 (258 economies); SP.POP.TOTL: total population, same period.

      Method

      Population without power is derived live: (100 − access%) × total population for the same year. Map shading, bars, rankings and every takeaway are computed from the API response on page load — no figure is hard-coded.
