    Data Sources & Methodology

    Atmospheric data (live)

    Our World in Data — annual & cumulative CO2 (Global Carbon Project), World entity, 1750–2024; temperature anomaly vs 1850–1900 baseline (HadCRUT5), 1850–2026. CC BY 4.0.

    COP record (curated)

    Conference hosts, presidencies and landmark decisions compiled from UNFCCC official records and public documentation, COP1–COP31. Embedded for instant load; update annually.

    Method

    Verdict lines are computed live from the data: CO2 growth in the 5 years after each COP is compared with the 5 years before it. The atmosphere, not the author, decides the verdict.
