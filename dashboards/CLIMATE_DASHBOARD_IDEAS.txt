IDEA 1 — "Decisions vs. the Atmosphere" — The COP Timeline [BUILDING NOW]
----------------------------------------------------------------
Sources: curated COP1-COP31 decisions dataset (embedded, instant load)
         + OWID CO2 (World entity, 1750-2024) + OWID temperature anomaly (live)
- Interactive timeline scrubber COP1 Berlin 1995 -> COP31 Antalya 2026
- Each COP: host city, presidency, landmark decisions (Kyoto 1997,
  Paris 2015, Loss & Damage Fund 2022, Global Stocktake 2023...)
- CO2 curve runs underneath with COP markers: did the curve bend
  after each decision? Auto-takeaways answer from live data.
- Mini-map flies to each host city.
Why it impresses: No official UNFCCC API exists (bot-walled) — a
structured, sourced COP dataset + live atmospheric data is something
even UNFCCC staff don't have in one place.

IDEA 2 — "275 Years of Carbon" — Who Emits, Who Emitted
----------------------------------------------------------------
Source: OWID annual CO2 per country (VERIFIED: 247 entities, 1750-2024)
- Choropleth + year slider 1750->2024 (reuse ten-years-of-flight engine):
  Industrial Revolution ignites Britain, US overtakes, China's 2000s surge
- Toggle: annual vs CUMULATIVE ("historical responsibility" — the #1
  negotiation fault line at every COP) vs per-capita
- Auto-takeaway: "The UK emitted more by 1900 than [country X]
  has in its entire history"
