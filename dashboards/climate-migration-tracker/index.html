      Data Sources & Methodology

      Refugees by origin (live)

      UNHCR Population Statistics — refugees by country of origin, 2015–2024, via the UNHCR Population Statistics API. Refugee figures are end-year stocks, not annual flows.

      Disaster displacement (live)

      World Bank Indicators API — VC.IDP.NWDS, new internal displacement associated with disasters (number of cases), published 2015–2023. 2024 has not been reported yet and is shown as a gap.

      Method

      Global disaster figures sum the country-level series only; World Bank regional and income aggregates are excluded to avoid double counting. All takeaways and rankings are computed live in the browser from the two APIs.
