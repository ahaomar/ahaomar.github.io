Independent practice · AI-assisted · live data

AI-assisted dashboards, built on official APIs, fetched at runtime.

A working series of interactive dashboards built against the public APIs of United Nations agencies and international organisations, designed, built and shipped with AI-assisted workflows under the same governance standards as the platform work. Each one fetches when you open it rather than shipping a snapshot, so the figures are the source’s figures, not mine.

The dashboards

  • World Bank Indicators API · UNHCR Population Statistics · 2015–2024

    The Aid Retreat: needs rise, money leaves

    Global aid sets records on paper while the displaced population multiplies faster. Total ODA plotted against people of concern to UNHCR, with aid per displaced person indexed to 2015. The two curves have never moved further apart.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · undernourishment · 2015–2023

    The Hunger Ledger: who is getting hungrier

    Undernourishment across 127 countries, year by year. Play the timeline and watch the worst entries change, then see the income-group lines separate: low-income hunger climbing while high-income hunger stays flat.

    Open dashboard

  • Our World in Data · Stanford AI Index · Epoch AI · 2013–2025

    The AI Chasm: who builds, who waits

    Private AI investment by region, cumulative large-scale AI systems by country, and AI's share of job postings. The capability to build frontier AI is concentrating in a handful of economies while everyone else consumes what those few produce.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · renewable freshwater · 2000–2022

    The Water Line: nations running dry

    Renewable freshwater per person, country by country, 2000 to 2022. The supply is a stock; population is a flow that compounds. Several nations have watched their water line halve within one generation.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · population & fertility · 2000–2023

    The Graying World: who ages first, who stays young

    Over-65 shares, old-age dependency and fertility extremes for every reporting country. Some economies doubled their over-65 share inside 23 years, others have not aged a day. The world is splitting in two.

    Open dashboard

  • Our World in Data · Global Carbon Project · 1750–2024

    275 Years of Carbon: who emits, who emitted

    Every tonne of CO₂ since the Industrial Revolution, by country. An animated year slider from 1750 to 2024 with annual, cumulative and per-capita views. The annual map shows who emits today, and the cumulative map inverts it into historical responsibility. That inversion is the whole argument of the piece.

    Open dashboard

  • UNFCCC COPs 1–31 · OWID live data · 1995–2026

    Decisions vs. the Atmosphere: 31 COPs, one curve

    Every COP since Berlin 1995, with host, presidency and landmark decisions, plotted against the CO₂ curve those decisions were meant to bend. Play through the summits and toggle annual, cumulative and temperature views to see whether the curve responded after Kyoto, Paris or Dubai.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · exposure × displacement · 1990–2023

    Too Hot to Stay: climate exposure × displacement

    162 countries on one risk map: climate exposure on one axis, disaster displacement on the other. The top-right quadrant is the crisis watchlist of tomorrow: Bangladesh, Somalia, Ethiopia already sit there. The CLIMB concept, built as a working product.

    Open dashboard

  • Our World in Data · Global Carbon Project · 1850–2024

    The Methane Moment: the forcer we keep ignoring

    Methane warms the planet 82× more than CO₂ over 20 years, then mostly breaks down within a decade. 175 years of methane data, animated, with top emitters, a two-clocks comparison against CO₂, and the short game of climate policy explained by the data.

    Open dashboard

  • Open-Meteo · Copernicus ERA5 · 1940–2025

    Is your city hotter than it used to be?

    Nobody lives in "global average." Search any city on Earth and compare its climate today with the 1940s, daily reanalysis data from the Copernicus ERA5 archive, fetched and analysed live in the browser. Type your hometown and watch the number.

    Open dashboard

  • UNHCR Population Statistics API · 2015–2024

    Ten Years of Flight: where refugees come from

    A decade of forced displacement, animated. Crises emerge, unfold and fade across the map: Ukraine 2022, Afghanistan 2021, and the long Syrian displacement, with per-country origin versus hosting analysis drawn live from the UNHCR API.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · 195 economies · 2018–2023

    The Invisible Lifeline: remittances against aid

    What migrant workers send home, set against official development assistance across 195 economies. A choropleth map and time series, with the comparison made directly rather than left for the reader to assemble.

    Open dashboard

  • World Bank Indicators API · disaster displacement · 2008–2024

    Displaced by Disaster: who extreme weather moves

    People forced from their homes by floods, storms and droughts. New disaster displacement across 195 economies, with per-country trends and climate-risk context: the dataset behind the climate-migration argument, without the rhetoric.

    Open dashboard

Why build these

The same techniques the UN platform work depends on.

Multi-source ingestion, provenance kept intact through the pipeline, and visual design that lets a policymaker reach a defensible reading quickly. These dashboards are where I keep those techniques sharp on data nobody commissioned.

They also make a point about verifiability: because each one reads from a public API at runtime, anyone can check a figure against the publisher. That is the same standard the CLIMB and Migration Data Portal work is held to.

See the platform work

Method

Live fetch, not snapshots
Each dashboard calls its source API on load. Nothing is baked in, so nothing silently goes stale.
AI-assisted, human-governed
Designed, coded and shipped with AI assistance under editorial control, the same human-in-the-loop governance as the UN platform work. Every figure traces to a public source.
Official sources only
UNHCR, UNFCCC, the World Bank and Our World in Data. No aggregators, no scraped intermediaries.
Self-contained
Each is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, the same constraint as the rest of this site.
