Fit

The problems worth writing to me about.

I am most useful where the technical problem and the institutional problem are the same problem — federated publishing across agencies with competing mandates, data that must keep its provenance through every transformation, platforms that have to survive a decade of staff turnover.

I am less useful as a pair of hands on a greenfield product with no governance constraints. That work is well served by people who enjoy it more than I do.

Good fit

Governance-scale platforms
Multi-agency or multi-tenant systems where publishing authority is distributed and conflicts are a design problem, not a bug.
Data-for-policy work
Multi-source ingestion, GIS layers, and visualisation intended for decision makers rather than analysts.
Long-horizon Drupal
Architecture that has to stay upgradable across major versions, with a custom surface that does not trap the institution.
Technical authority roles
Architect-of-record positions where the decision and the accountability sit together.
