Independent practice

Dashboards built on live official data.

A working series of interactive dashboards built against the public APIs of United Nations agencies and international organisations — UNHCR, UNFCCC, the World Bank, Our World in Data. Each one fetches at runtime rather than shipping a snapshot, so the figures are the source’s figures, not mine.

These are the same techniques the CLIMB and Migration Data Portal work depends on: multi-source ingestion, provenance kept intact through the pipeline, and visual design that lets a policymaker reach a defensible reading quickly.
